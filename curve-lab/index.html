Try Circle: its parameterization has constant speed, so aT = 0 and the acceleration is entirely normal. Try Line (accelerating): aN = 0 and the acceleration is entirely tangential. On the constant-speed line, both components are 0.

6 · Second curve in the moving TNB frame

q(t) = r(t) + a(t)T(t) + b(t)N(t) + c(t)B(t)
The coefficient formulas can use t and s, where s is approximate arc length measured from min t. This makes uniform-distance spirals possible, e.g. 0.5*cos(4*s).

7 · Appearance & thickness

Thickness is rendered as actual 3D geometry, so it remains visible consistently across browsers and projectors.

8 · View, window & projection

Ride Curve switches to an elevated first-person perspective attached to the Frenet frame: forward = T and up = B. Drag to look around without changing your riding position; scroll in for a closer first-person view or scroll out to pull behind the moving TNB frame for a chase view. Frenet vectors become undefined where r′ is zero or where curvature vanishes; the app flags those points rather than inventing a frame.
