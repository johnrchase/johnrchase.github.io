Version history

What's new in Total Teacher Toolkit.

Version 2.0.1

In development · Updated November 24, 2026

  • Seating warnings. Include unmet priority-seat requirements in the toolbar warning alert, alongside grouping-rule conflicts. Show the combined count in its tooltip and list affected students in the details.
  • Current/all exports. Provide separate Export current seating chart and Export all seating charts options, plus equivalent room-layout options. Current exports contain one item and use its name; all seating charts includes saved charts and working drafts across classes. Keep student data out of room exports. One import action accepts either single-item or multi-item files.
  • Together-group placement. Randomize eligible table choices for students required to sit together, instead of repeatedly choosing the first table. Retain locks, together/apart rules, priority-seat preference, and front-first filling where possible. Add repeatable regression checks for varied placements and front-table preference.

Version 2.0

In development · Updated November 24, 2026

  • Total Teacher Toolkit 2.0. Rename the app and introduce a matching amber T-cubed briefcase logo and favicon. Put the brand and shared controls above workspace navigation and saved-class controls. Selected workspace and First/Last/Both buttons use black. Keep shared controls equally sized, remove redundant labels, and separate persistent navigation visually from each workspace toolbar. Retain the old app address as a redirect and preserve existing browser data and backup compatibility.
  • Groups & Tools toolbar. Combine Randomize!, Display, Jigsaw, quick image copy, Export, group count, target size, group names, member labels, their settings gear, and Group Colors into one compact row. Generate group names when a theme is selected. Keep Jigsaw and Un-jigsaw equally wide, align the numeric fields, and retain expandable Activity Instructions below. Move students and groups by dragging; temporarily remove and restore individuals or entire groups.
  • Member labels. Offer numbers, letters, shapes, Greek, animals, flags, faces, foods, or no labels. The gear selects eligible symbols, with Select all and at least one retained option. Remember choices in class settings, JSON backups, and pop-outs. Shuffle eligible symbol sets on ordinary page loads while keeping labels consistent across groups, Jigsaw, display, and exports. Bundle detailed flag artwork locally, including El Salvador; use country names in text exports.
  • Classroom layouts. Separate roster-independent Room Designer from class-specific Seating Chart. Maintain independent room drafts, reusable named layouts, working charts, and undo histories. Select saved layouts directly; use Rename, New, Save, Save Copy, and confirmed Delete. Copies have unique Copy of names. Show Saved! after File & Export until another edit. Disable class and roster controls in Room Designer to make its independence clear.
  • Layout templates. New opens configurable Portrait, Landscape, Square, and Custom templates: empty rooms, desk grids, stadium seating, horseshoes, discussion circles, table groups, and joined-desk groups. Support rows and columns up to eight, seats or desks per group, one-sided forward-facing seating, and multiple horseshoe or circle layers. Divide total desks across layers with rounding up. Open new and loaded layouts at Fit room.
  • Room editing. Use a full-width, full-height canvas with clean wall corners and a collapsible two-column Objects/Edit tray. Adding furniture keeps Objects open; selecting opens Edit without expanding a collapsed tray, while double-click expands it. Support multi-selection, grouping, table groups, layers, copy/cut/paste/duplicate, keyboard shortcuts, wheel zoom, panning, resizing, and center-preserving rotation. Teacher/student views keep labels readable and arrow movement screen-relative. Grid and object snapping can be toggled separately.
  • Furniture and finishes. Customize furniture colors, borders, textures, fonts, and label emphasis; object labels start hidden. Add desks, chairs, tables, office and lounge seating, boards, shelves, doors, windows, lamps, plants, rugs, cabinets, sinks, stovetops, book carts, attached desk-chairs, computers, keyboards, laptops, printers, and aligned school supplies. Use top-view artwork, continuous wood grain, subdued recolorable rug patterns, stretchable oval rugs, and compact parallel chair casters. Drag doors into walls to create openings.
  • Seating assignment. Create charts from the current class roster, with named charts restricted to that class. Changing layouts clears stale chart selections without overwriting saved charts. Drag students onto chairs and occupied chairs to swap; empty chairs stay visually empty. Sync the roster, remove students, or clear assignments. Names follow shared First/Last/Both and font controls, with tight boxes and uniform scaling. Physical chairs can be marked unavailable; office chairs and lounge furniture start unavailable by default.
  • Randomization and preferences. The settings gear controls empty-seat labels, seat-number display, and whether randomization respects grouping rules, empty seats, student priority preferences, and locks. Respecting empty seats shuffles only occupied chairs; disabling it lets Randomize use chairs throughout the room. Fill supports random, surname, and given-name orders with front-first placement. Shuffle within tables preserves table membership. Store priority preferences per student in the Students tray, and priority seat locations in Room Designer; keep public views free of private markings.
  • Rules and feedback. Share Grouping rules beside Edit names. Together means one table or conceptual table group; apart means different tables or groups. Constrained fills report impossible combinations rather than silently breaking rules. Manual moves remain possible and show warnings below working-chart guidance. Locks and priority preferences can be temporarily ignored during randomization without deleting their saved settings. Report capacity shortages and unmet priority preferences. Collapse the Students list when more canvas space is needed.
  • Seat numbers. Keep numbering in Room Designer beside Room Settings; scale number badges and their text with A-/A+. Offer rows, columns, alternating snakes from either side, and clockwise numbering around tables. Drag one number badge onto another to swap just those two numbers without moving furniture; individual seat numbers can also be edited. Preserve numbering with the layout. Seating Chart only controls visibility, and enabled numbers carry into class displays and image exports.
  • Guidance and accessibility. Standardize toolbar controls, spacing, and save feedback; place class save status and matching right-aligned warning emojis with count tooltips and clickable details in the Groups and Seating Chart toolbars, move Group Colors beside Show tools, and center seating save feedback against the toolbar buttons; distinguish named saves from stored working drafts and report storage failures. Add actionable empty states, canvas drop and wall-snap feedback, visible keyboard focus, dialog focus containment/return, keyboard menus, and reduced-motion support. Provide searchable contextual Help with workspace guides, shortcuts, troubleshooting, privacy, backups, and mobile guidance. Add a substitute-teacher printout with chart preview, date, perspective, a Teacher view default, optional full-name roster table with four blank attendance/data columns identifying unseated students, and optional notes; omit private priority/rule/lock details.
  • Room presentation and export. Use a single Teacher/Student view toggle beside Pan. Offer a default-on Show layout title option in Seating Chart settings, remembered with each chart and honored by displays and exports. Use the blue primary Save button in Room Designer. Display for class fills the screen without editing settings and allows quick perspective switching. Use matching Export dropdown buttons in all workspaces and explain disabled controls with contextual tooltips. Export offers clipboard PNG, PNG/SVG downloads, and Print / Save PDF from both workspaces. Keep outer image margins transparent while retaining an opaque floor. Name room and seating downloads after the current layout or chart. Label Room Designer JSON actions Export room layout and Import room layout. Save room-only backups separately from class seating data; imports merge reusable layouts and saved charts.
  • Group history. Add Undo/Redo beside Jigsaw for student moves, randomization, group order, names, removals/restores, sizing, and Jigsaw changes. Support Ctrl/Cmd+Z, Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+Z, and Ctrl/Cmd+Y outside text fields; keep history separate from room/seating edits, clear it when switching classes or rosters, and discard redo after a new group change.
  • Groups image export. Put image export first in Export. Export Groups Image provides centered Copy PNG, Download PNG, Download JPG, and Print actions above a live preview. Adjust columns, spacing, font size, and font family without presets; scale the class title with the font slider. Optionally include borders, colors, group names, member labels, instructions, and highlights. Omit periods after member labels. Place excluded people in a neutral, unlabelled Removed box. Menu Print opens this same dialog.
  • Classroom tools. Provide random picker, countdown timer, stopwatch, Community Builder, and local-time clock. Give pop-outs compact tool tabs, aligned controls, responsive graphics, and larger names and digital displays. Stopwatch fractional-seconds off hides both tenths and hundredths; its analog face appears before Start. Community questions scale with the window and support Previous question. Remember tool order, visibility, styles, backgrounds, and relevant preferences; hide move handles in enlarged and pop-out views.
  • Appearance and mobile. Offer dark mode, fullscreen, font controls, and a one-click inline background color picker with rainbow hue, saturation/brightness, hex input, and Reset. Use a default-on Show tools checkbox to hide the desktop dock and expand groups. On phones, provide Groups and Tools tabs, full-width readable group cards, touch-sized controls, and one tool at a time. Offer an in-app Desktop version switch for the full interface, including Seating Chart and Room Designer.
  • Data and verification. Save working state in the current browser and provide current-class, all-class, and seating JSON exports/imports. Treat full backups as teacher-only because they include rosters, preferences, locks, and assignments. Pictures and class displays omit private seating information. Keep locally bundled artwork and license notices with the app. Validate seating behavior with focused engine tests and browser checks across desktop, pop-outs, phone layouts, saved data, and exports; committing does not publish a release.
  • Object details. Use Bold, Italic, Underline, label rotation, layer movement, and visibly disabled unsupported properties in the object editor. Numbered and assignable seats remain distinct from decorative furniture. Give the couch three optional places, the comfy chair one, and office chairs optional student assignments. Add a small metal trashcan with an Empty-to-Overflowing slider, visible paper, cardboard, and banana peels; the default is half full. A skinny blue recycling bin uses bundled licensed recycling artwork.
  • Working efficiently. Keep contextual room settings and file menus floating over the canvas, without shifting the page. Use compact zoom presets without accumulating wheel-generated percentages, and let Fit room show the whole room without an inner canvas scrollbar. Keep the object count and navigation shortcuts in a status bar. Maintain unique table-group names when duplicating, and keep attached chairs aligned when moving, rotating, or resizing their parent furniture.
  • Sharing and recovery. Quick image copy uses the latest export settings. Keep the copy-share-link, text export, image export, current-class export, all-class export, and import actions together. Class display includes optional activity instructions and highlighted students. Transparent PNG and opaque white JPEG backgrounds remain deliberate choices. Download filenames use Total Teacher Toolkit while machine-readable legacy format identifiers and browser storage keys stay compatible with existing installations.

Version 1.3

In development · Not yet released · Updated October 22, 2026

  • Separate countdown and stopwatch tools: larger digital displays, stopwatch tenths, and analog views. Countdown also offers a rounded glass sand timer and animated dynamite with a traveling fuse flame and explosion, plus optional matching alarm sounds.
  • Consistent sidebar headers for all four tools, with a left collapse arrow and right enlarge/restore, pop-out, and drag controls. Remember collapsed panels and tool order; Alt + arrow keys also reorder tools.
  • Community builder includes 270 supplied icebreaker questions, a daily question, and a centered new-question button. Question text follows the app font-size controls.
  • Read-only Display for class sits in the top toolbar and shows groups with teacher-written activity instructions above them. Suggested instructions append directly to the text box below settings.
  • Choose numbers, letters, or no member labels beside Show names; labels carry through classroom display and exports.
  • Picker avoids recent repeats even when repeats are allowed, or cycles through all eligible choices when disabled. One member from every group now chooses independently within each group. Selected students are highlighted, with a subtle centered Clear highlights control.
  • Image and text exports and share links can optionally include activity instructions and highlighted members. Both options start off and remember preferences via cookies.
  • New preferences and instructions persist between visits and are included in JSON backups. Running clocks and random-pick history are session-only; popped-out clocks continue from their current time.
  • Fresh random groups on each page load, while preserving saved classes, absent students, grouping rules, and settings. Centered community questions, lighter move handles, and selection highlights that contain the student controls.
